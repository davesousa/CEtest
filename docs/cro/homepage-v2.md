## Verdict: Ship-ready
The live `/v2` homepage makes a coherent, luxury-appropriate case for a private fitting: identity and aesthetic first, credibility and process next, then a clear appointment invitation.

## Findings

### Consider: Preserve the first-viewport conversion hierarchy
- **Route to:** accept-as-is
- **What I observed:** The hero opens with `C|E Clothier · Toronto`, the headline `Driven by passion, delivered with style.`, the bespoke-garment supporting line, and a fully written `Book a private fitting` CTA over a directed client image. At the mobile viewport, the compact header `BOOK` action remains visible and the full hero CTA is present within the initial composition.
- **Why it hurts conversion:** No current harm observed. The composition establishes what the house is, who it is for, and the desired next step without resorting to retail-style urgency or clutter.
- **Recommendation:** Keep this hierarchy intact. Do not add badges, service links, scheduling detail, or additional proof into the hero; each would weaken the singular private-fitting invitation.

### Consider: Preserve claim-to-proof adjacency
- **Route to:** accept-as-is
- **What I observed:** The founder-positioned philosophy claim is followed directly by `Recognized in the pages of` and the GQ, Vogue, Vanity Fair, and Us Weekly marks before the craft section begins.
- **Why it hurts conversion:** No current harm observed. This placement answers the credibility question at the moment a first-time visitor is asked to accept the house's point of view, rather than deferring proof until after several additional sections.
- **Recommendation:** Retain the press band immediately after philosophy and keep its framing claim limited to editorial recognition. Do not relocate it toward the terminal CTA or expand it into a logo grid.

### Consider: Preserve proof before the terminal ask
- **Route to:** accept-as-is
- **What I observed:** Before the final appointment prompt, the live sequence substantiates bespoke value through individual pattern-making, posture and movement, a library of more than 1,000 fabrics, considered details, a full-scale finished-look editorial image, and four clear wardrobe disciplines.
- **Why it hurts conversion:** No current harm observed. The page gives a skeptical, high-consideration visitor concrete reasons to believe the work is bespoke before asking for an appointment, while reserving pricing and lead-time detail for the process route as intended.
- **Recommendation:** Keep the craft section free of booking interruptions. Maintain `Discover our approach` as the quiet route for visitors who need more process detail before committing.

### Consider: Preserve the CTA hierarchy and terminal close
- **Route to:** accept-as-is
- **What I observed:** `Book a private fitting` is the unmistakable hero action; secondary paths are explicitly navigational (`Discover our approach`, `Explore the work`, and the discipline rows); and the page ends with the largest booking treatment, `Book your private fitting`, alongside `Toronto, Ontario · Private studio · By appointment`.
- **Why it hurts conversion:** No current harm observed. The conversion path is repeated at sensible decision points without turning the page into a persistent DTC-style booking prompt, and the terminal logistics reduce the practical uncertainty of an appointment.
- **Recommendation:** Keep the terminal CTA as the only full-weight booking moment. Avoid a sticky bottom booking bar; it would add conversion pressure at the cost of the brand's editorial restraint.
